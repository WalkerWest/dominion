Who it's for

Built for regional enterprises
that have outgrown cloud economics.

Not for hyperscaler customers. Not for bare-metal renters. For the ML team at a regional manufacturer, hospital system, or mid-market SaaS company that is spending $50–500K/month on AWS GPU and is ready for a better option.

Cloud GPU spend is your #2 line item
Sustained inference and training workloads are expensive on AWS, Azure, and GCP. Dominion's on-site generation eliminates the power cost that drives cloud GPU pricing. The economics improve with utilization.
Data residency is non-negotiable
Healthcare, financial services, and government clients need workloads to stay in the region. Dominion's campus infrastructure is located in the same market as you, operated by a community institution, with a contractual data residency commitment.
You have ML capability, not ops capacity
Your data science team knows how to build models. They should not be managing Kubernetes clusters, GPU drivers, and storage provisioning. Dominion handles the infrastructure. You bring the containers.
Predictable cost, dedicated capacity
No spot instance volatility. No preemption. Committed monthly tiers with dedicated GPU nodes — your workloads run when you need them. A human account manager answers the phone.

Federation Compute Exchange

Campus capacity, shared
across the network.

Dominion operates multiple campuses across the Midwest. When one campus has spare capacity and another has a spike in demand, the federation exchange routes workloads automatically. Every campus benefits from the utilization economics of the full network.

Spot
Real-time availability
Spare capacity listed by the hour. Claim it for training runs or burst inference. Low commitment, high flexibility.
Floor rate set by federation governance · hourly settlement
Forward
Scheduled capacity
Reserve burst capacity in advance. Campus gets revenue certainty; you get availability certainty for planned training runs.
Typically 15–20% below spot · reserved settlement
Agreement
Long-term sharing
Formal capacity sharing agreement between two campuses. Recurring availability at the best rates — like a peering agreement for compute.
Typically 25–35% below spot · quarterly settlement
